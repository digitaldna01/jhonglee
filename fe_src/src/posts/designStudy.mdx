<Lang locale="en">

A studybook of six design principles — contrast, texture, symmetry, asymmetry, repetition,
and balance — working from Ellen Lupton and Jennifer Cole Phillips's
_Graphic Design: The New Basics_.

Honestly, I wasn't confident going in. I'm not unusually creative, and I don't have a better
eye than anyone else — I just liked design. The people in that class were genuinely creative.
Looking back, I think what I really liked was simply making things alongside people like that.

</Lang>

<Lang locale="ko">

디자인의 여섯 원리, 대비·질감·대칭·비대칭·반복·균형을 하나씩 공부하며 만든 스터디북이에요.
교재는 Ellen Lupton과 Jennifer Cole Phillips의 \_Graphic Design: The New Basics.

솔직히 처음엔 자신이 없었어요. 창의력이 남다른 것도, 남들보다 눈이 좋은 것도 아니고, 그냥
디자인이 좋았을 뿐이거든요. 같이 수업 듣던 친구들은 정말로 창의적이었어요. 지금 생각하면
저는 그런 사람들 틈에서 뭔가를 만든다는 것 자체가 좋았던 것 같아요.

</Lang>

<Lang locale="en">

One line from the textbook made it into the book itself — Rick Valicenti's epigraph on
texture: "If you touch something (it is likely) someone will feel it. If you feel something
(it is likely) someone will be touched."
