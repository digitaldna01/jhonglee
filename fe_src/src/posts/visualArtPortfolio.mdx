*Pencil on paper*

A tomato cradled in a work glove, a Coca-Cola can, cucumbers, aluminum foil spread underneath — everyday things rearranged on the page and drawn with a single pencil. The chase was the mass and shading a single light source from the right gives each shape. The foil is there on purpose: nothing splits light into more pieces inside one object.

*Acrylic on canvas*

I landed in the US at fifteen with barely any English, and my first home was Austin's skaters. The language of skating was enough — and hanging around them is how my ears finally opened. Skate parks are cement and rebar; through my eyes they looked this loud and dynamic. The body is drawn in detail and the face left undefined, a self still changing between two cultures — and the blunt "POP IT!" borrows the grammar of pop art.
</div>

*Acrylic on canvas*

I believe experience is what shapes a person — character, goals, even the way you talk. So the cities that shaped me are pressed into one landscape, starting at the Han River and running through Boston with its CITGO sign, Rochester, Austin, Niagara Falls, Monument Valley. The buildings stand clean-edged, cut with masking tape; the river runs in rough brushstrokes. Hard edges and loose paint sharing one frame — which is what the journey was.
</div>

### DMT (Dive Myself To)
</Lang>

<div className="exhibit wide">
  <img src="/images/projects/visual-art-portfolio/dmt.jpg" alt="" />
</div>

<Lang locale="en">
<div className="placard">
*Acrylic on canvas*

This one comes out of my two years of military service back in Korea. The forced pause became a second chance to plan ahead, and one resolution came out of it: be the helmsman, not another oar. My unit mates and I spent every free hour on our own crafts — dance, music, painting, code — and we called the habit DMT: Do My Thing, follow my own interests instead of the current. The color blooming upward is how forward that time felt, and the circles, large and small, are me diving into a world of my own.
</div>
</Lang>
<Lang locale="ko">
<div className="placard">
*캔버스에 아크릴*

군 복무로 한국에 돌아와 있던 2년에서 나온 그림이에요. 강제로 멈춘 그 시간에 정한 게 하나 있어요. 노를 젓는 사람 말고 키를 잡는 사람이 되자. 부대 동료들과 자유 시간마다 각자 춤, 음악, 그림, 코드에 몰두했고, 그 습관을 DMT라고 불렀어요. Do My Thing, 유행이 아니라 내 관심사를 따라가기. 번져 오르는 색은 앞을 내다보던 그때의 기분이고, 크고 작은 원들은 제 세계로 다이브하는 저예요.
</div>
