I landed in the US at fifteen with barely any English, and the skate park took me in first — skaters became a second family, skating a language that needed no translating. The cities that shaped me, pressed into one landscape: the Han River, Boston and its CITGO sign, Rochester, Austin, Niagara Falls, Monument Valley. ### DMT (Dive Myself To)
</Lang>

<div className="exhibit wide">
  <img src="/images/projects/visual-art-portfolio/dmt.jpg" alt="" />
</div>

<Lang locale="en">
<div className="placard">
*Acrylic on canvas*

This one comes out of my two years of military service in Korea. In my unit I found people who spent every free hour on their own thing — dance, music, painting, code — and we named the habit DMT: Do My Thing, dive myself into. The color climbs from murky to bright the way that time felt, and the circles, large and small, are me diving into a world of my own.
</div>
</Lang>
<Lang locale="ko">
<div className="placard">
*캔버스에 아크릴*

이 그림은 한국에서 군 복무한 2년에서 나왔어요. 부대에서 자유 시간마다 각자의 것에 몰두하는 사람들을 만났어요. 춤, 음악, 그림, 코드. 우리는 그 습관을 DMT라고 불렀어요. Do My Thing, 그리고 Dive myself into. 탁한 색에서 밝은 색으로 올라오는 건 그 시절의 기분이고, 크고 작은 원들은 제 세계로 뛰어드는 저예요.
</div>
