## Music

My favorite artist is Travis Scott — him and the whole Cactus Jack orbit,
Don Toliver to Sheck Wes. The other rail is Korean hip-hop, deep enough that
a whole Sik-K × Lil Moshpit era sits in my all-time top 50, with B.I and GD
alongside. Dijon and Mac Miller are what I reach for when I want it softer.

## Photography

My main hobby is photography — photo walks with friends, mostly landscapes and
people. The gear tells its own story: I started on a Sony Alpha 7R, moved to a
Leica D-Lux, and a Canon V1 is next. The latest roll is from a trip to
Thailand — Bangkok's Grand Palace and the brick ruins of Ayutthaya along the
river.

## Travel

Ten countries so far. Korea and the US I've lived in; then Japan (a solo trip
to Osaka and Kyoto), Hong Kong, Thailand, the UK, France, Spain, and Italy —
those last four in one three-week run from London through Paris, Barcelona,
Granada, and Sevilla to Rome. My favorite is the one that isn't like the
others: Georgia, where I lived for a month over the winter of 2024–25.

## Sports

The gym is my routine; lacrosse was my sport in high school, and the team
shirt is still in rotation.

## Weekends

Weekends are photo walks, the gym, and friends — billiards, a jjimjilbang
(찜질방) run, nogari (노가리, dried pollack) and draft beer at an
Euljiro (을지로) style pub.

## Small favorites

My drink is Dr Pepper — the people who know me well know that much. It even
made it into one of my drawings: the Inner Circle placard measures closeness
by who knows I'll always reach for one.
