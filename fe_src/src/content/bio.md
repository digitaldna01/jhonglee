## Background

I studied Computer Science at Boston University with a minor in Visual Arts,
including a visiting year in Computer Science & Engineering at Korea University.
My technical background spans algorithm design, AI and machine learning, and
quantum computing; the visual side spans typography, drawing, and motion.

## Work

I work as a software engineer at LG CNS in Seoul on smart factory systems.
Before that I worked on machine-learning problems at LSAT Lab and LSAT Demon
(time-series deep learning and LLM development), did undergraduate computer
vision research in Boston University's IVC group, and spent two research
internships at the Korea Electronics Technology Institute on quantum computing
and computer vision. I co-authored a publication on machine-learning-based hand
pose generation using a haptic controller.

## How I work

I like building the whole seam: designing an interface and then building what
runs underneath it, models included. This site is an example — the landing page
embeds your question, retrieves from my own writing, and answers with citations
back to the source. Precision and craft over spectacle.
