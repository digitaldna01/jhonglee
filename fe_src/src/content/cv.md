## How to contact me

The best way to contact me is email: ll.leejaehong.ll@gmail.com. You can also
find me on GitHub (github.com/digitaldna01), LinkedIn
(linkedin.com/in/hong-lee-0821) and Instagram (@8.21j). This site is
jhonglee.com, and a PDF résumé is on its CV page.

## Education

I studied at Boston University from September 2019 to May 2025 and graduated
with a B.A. in Computer Science and a minor in Visual Arts, magna cum laude
with a 3.90 GPA, and was inducted into Upsilon Pi Epsilon, the computing honor
society. During that time I spent a visiting year in Computer Science &
Engineering at Korea University in Seoul (September 2022 – June 2023). Before
university I went to high school at Aquinas Institute of Rochester in
Rochester, NY (2016–2019) and Bowie High School in Austin, TX (2015–2016).

## Experience

I work at LG CNS in Seoul as an agentic AI software engineer on smart factory
systems, since January 2026. There I built a retrieval-augmented generation
system for MES manuals: preprocessing pipelines (PDF parsing, semantic
chunking, metadata injection), embeddings stored in pgvector, and LLM-based
query processing — rewriting, hybrid BM25 + vector retrieval, reranking — in a
modular FastAPI backend. Before that I was a machine learning specialist at
LSAT Lab (September–December 2025, remote), working on time-series deep
learning, and at LSAT Demon (June–September 2025, remote), where I developed
an LLM-powered backend for personalized text generation with semantic
retrieval (BM25, FAISS), curated domain datasets, and did the prompt
engineering for deployment on models such as Mistral and GPT. My first
internship was at Xenix Studio in Seoul (July–November 2020), as a software
engineer intern on blockchain and frontend work.

## Research

At Boston University I was an undergraduate research assistant in computer
vision with the IVC group (April 2024 – May 2025), advised by Aoming Liu, on
text-to-360-degree panorama generation: a cube-based approach combining spot
diffusion with latent-space noise projection to improve depth and visual
continuity. Before that (September 2023 – May 2024), advised by Eung-Joo Lee
and Jong In Choi, I worked on AI-driven hand pose estimation with a custom
haptic controller, training an XGBoost model on 3,000–9,000 samples — the
work behind the Hand Pose Estimation project and the publication below.

I spent two summers as a research intern at the Korea Electronics Technology
Institute (KETI) in Seongnam. In 2024, on system optimization, I contributed to
research on memory-efficient quantum simulation that stores only the measured
qubits, up to 45 qubits, built a quantum circuit simulator with Google's
TensorNetwork, Qiskit and a statevector engine to run Grover's algorithm, and
gave two internal seminars on qubits, superposition, Bell's theorem and the
EPR paradox. In 2022, on computer vision, I applied Google's Vision
Transformer to cat and dog breed classification, reaching over 95% accuracy,
and re-implemented the model in TensorFlow with an API and an OpenCV camera
module for real-time classification of real pets.

## Other projects
